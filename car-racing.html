About 3D Car Racing by Mahsua Apps

3D Car Racing by Mahsua Apps brings Play Store-style 3D highway traffic racing straight to your browser. Inspect 5 custom 3D vehicles in the interactive Showroom Garage, unlock exotic supercars, and weave through four lanes of dynamic highway traffic with near-miss combo bonuses.

Touch Swipe & Keyboard Controls

  • Mobile & Tablet (No On-Screen Buttons): Swipe Left/Right to change lanes, Swipe Up (or double-tap) to fire Nitro Boost, Swipe Down to brake.
  • Desktop Keyboard: Use ← → or A D to steer lanes, ↑ or Space for Nitro Boost, and ↓ or S to brake.
