About Crossword Puzzle by Mahsua Apps

Crossword Puzzle by Mahsua Apps is a free, browser-friendly word puzzle built for instant play on any device. Sharpen your vocabulary across 30 levels each in Easy (8×8), Medium (10×10), and Hard (12×12) modes.

How to Play

Tap any white square or click a clue from the Across or Down lists to highlight the word. Type letters using your physical keyboard or the built-in on-screen keyboard. Tap the same square again to switch between Across and Down, and use Hint or Check anytime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it 100% free?

Yes—play all 90 crossword levels online at zero cost with no app installation.

How do levels work?

Easy (8×8), Medium (10×10), and Hard (12×12) each include 30 unique puzzles with saved progress.

Works on mobile?

Yes, it adapts to any screen size and includes a touch-friendly on-screen keyboard.
