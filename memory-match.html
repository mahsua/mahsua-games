About Memory Match by Mahsua Apps

Memory Match by Mahsua Apps is a free, lightweight concentration and visual recall game built for mobile, tablet, and desktop browsers. Progress through 30 levels each across Easy (12 cards), Medium (16 cards), and Hard (20 cards) with five vibrant icon themes.

How to Play

Tap any two cards to flip them face-up and find matching pairs. Consecutive matches trigger combo streak multipliers for higher scores and 3-star ratings. Need a quick boost? Use Peek Hint to briefly reveal unmatched cards, or unlock levels directly from the level selector.
