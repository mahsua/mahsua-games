About Word Search by Mahsua Apps

Word Search by Mahsua Apps is a free, lightweight browser puzzle game built for instant play on mobile, tablet, and desktop. Progress through 30 handcrafted levels in each difficulty—Easy (8×8), Medium (10×10), and Hard (12×12)—across Technology, Nature, Space, Food & Drinks, and Everyday themes.

How to Play

Find every hidden word from the list by dragging your finger or mouse across the letters in any straight line—horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. You can also tap the first letter and then tap the last letter. Stuck on a word? Use the Hint button to highlight its starting letter, and unlock all 30 levels in every mode.
